About Scientific Finishing School

Scientific Finishing School is a dedicated Matric Rewrite centre located at Roskrudge Building, Lusikisiki. We give learners a second chance to improve their matric results and open doors to university, college and bursary opportunities. We offer a wide range of subjects including Mathematics, Maths Literacy, Physical Sciences, Life Sciences, Agricultural Sciences, Geography, Economics, Business Studies, Accounting and English FAL. With experienced teachers, focused exam preparation, and accommodation available for learners from afar, we create a supportive environment where you can awake and pursue your future. Classes for 2027 start in January 2027, with exams in May/June and October/November 2027.
